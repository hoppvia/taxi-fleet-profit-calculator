UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

How to calculate taxi fleet operating profit

Start with gross customer bookings: active drivers × trips per driver per day × days operated × average fare. Subtract the amounts paid to drivers, payment processing fees, other per-trip costs and fixed monthly expenses.

The formula

Monthly operating result = (drivers × trips/day × days × average fare) − driver payouts − payment fees − per-trip costs − fixed costs.

What break-even means

Break-even trips per driver per day is the minimum average activity needed to cover the monthly fixed costs, under the assumed per-trip contribution. If per-trip contribution is zero or negative, no finite trip volume will cover fixed expenses.

Common questions

Does the calculator use real taxi market averages?

No. The starting values are clearly illustrative, not independent market research or forecasts. Replace every assumption with your operation's actual numbers.

Are the software cost figures Hoppvia's prices?

No. The software input is yours to edit. For current Hoppvia options and a quote appropriate to your fleet, refer to Hoppvia's pricing page.

Can I use this for London, New York or Dubai?

Yes. Select GBP, USD or AED as appropriate, then enter local fares, driver payouts and costs. The calculator does not estimate local taxes or regulatory expenses automatically.

Is anything sent to a server?

No calculation inputs are transmitted to a backend by this tool. The optional share-link feature puts the entered values into the URL, so anyone with that link can read them. Use an ordinary reset before sharing publicly if your figures are sensitive.
